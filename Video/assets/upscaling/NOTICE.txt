Full licenses are bundled in upscaling/licenses. SOURCE-SHA256.txt identifies
all pinned original sources. Video/shaders/generate_upscaling.py reproducibly
wraps the mpv shader bodies, stores the LUTs as half-float texture data, and
adapts SGSR1 to GLES 3.1 / constant gather component / legal constructor syntax.
SGSR1 runs Qualcomm's supported RGBY mode with luma in alpha, original threshold
8/255 and sharpness 2.0. Its adaptive reconstruction math is unchanged.
FSRCNNX's trained shader is unchanged; Nova's separate chroma combination pass
limits luma overshoot to 2% of the local source envelope to prevent edge halos.
